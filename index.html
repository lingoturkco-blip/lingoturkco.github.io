About Lingo Turkco

Lingo Turkco is an online English learning platform created especially for Turkish speakers who want to learn English in a simple and practical way.

Our lessons combine Turkish explanations with real English examples, making vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation and speaking much easier to understand.

Whether you are a complete beginner or an advanced learner, Lingo Turkco helps you improve your English step by step with confidence.
