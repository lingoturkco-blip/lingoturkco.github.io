About Lingo Turkco

Lingo Turkco is an online English learning platform designed especially for Turkish speakers.

Our mission is to make learning English easier through Turkish explanations, clear examples and practical lessons.

Whether you are a beginner or an advanced learner, Lingo Turkco helps you improve your vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, speaking, writing and listening skills.
